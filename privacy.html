Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: September 2026

Hirsch & Co. Accounting LLC ("Hirsch CPA," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website; the confidential information you share with us as a client in the course of our professional services is also protected by our professional obligations described below.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us when you email us, call us, or write to us. That is usually your name, contact details, and whatever you tell us about what you need.

This website itself does not ask you for any information. It has no working form, it does not set cookies, it does not use analytics, and it does not store anything on your device. You can read every page on this site without giving us anything at all.

As with any website, our hosting provider keeps standard technical records of requests made to the server, such as the address your request came from and the page you asked for. That is part of delivering the site and keeping it secure.

Our calculators

The calculators on this site run entirely inside your own browser. The figures you type are used to work out the answer on your screen and nothing more: they are not sent to us, they are not saved, and we never see them. Closing the page clears them.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and communicate with you
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services and our website
  • To schedule and follow up on calls and appointments
  • To meet our legal, professional, and regulatory obligations

Client confidentiality

As Certified Public Accountants, we are bound by professional standards of confidentiality. Financial and tax information you provide to us as a client is kept confidential and is used only to provide the services you have engaged us for, except as required or permitted by law. Documents made available through our secure client portal are protected by access controls and encryption.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use cookies on this website, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools. Nothing follows you between pages, and nothing is shared with advertisers. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business, such as our hosting and email providers, and only to the extent needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your information using appropriate technical and organizational protections.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold, to correct it, or to ask us to delete anything we are not required to keep. We do not run a mailing list and this website does not sign you up to anything.

Children's privacy

Our website is intended for a general, professional audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The client portal

The Client Login link takes you to our secure client portal, which is a separate site with its own sign in. Documents there are protected by access controls and encryption, and only the people connected to your account can reach them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date above.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at info@hirsch.cpa, call (347) 486-2780, or write to us at 65 S 11th Street, Brooklyn, NY.
