Tax Return Due Dates

Key federal deadlines for calendar-year filers. When a date falls on a weekend or holiday, it moves to the next business day.

Filing deadlines
March 15S corporations & partnershipsForms 1120-S & 1065
April 15Individuals, C corporations, trusts & estatesForms 1040, 1120 & 1041
May 15Tax-exempt organizationsForms 990 / 990-EZ / 990-PF
On extension
September 15S corporations & partnerships
September 30Trusts & estates
October 15Individuals & C corporations
November 15Tax-exempt organizations
Also good to know: Estate tax (Form 706) is due 9 months after the date of death, with a 6-month extension available. An extension gives you more time to file, not more time to pay, any tax owed is still due by the original deadline. Fiscal-year filers and June-30 C corporations follow different dates.
