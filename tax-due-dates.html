Tax Return Due Dates

Key federal deadlines for calendar-year filers. When a date falls on a weekend or holiday, it moves to the next business day.

Individuals

ReturnFormDue dateExtended
Individual income tax1040April 15October 15

Businesses

ReturnFormDue dateExtended
S corporation1120-SMarch 15September 15
Partnership / multi-member LLC1065March 15September 15
C corporation (calendar year)1120April 15October 15

Trusts, Estates & Nonprofits

ReturnFormDue dateExtended
Estates & trusts income tax1041April 15September 30
Estate tax7069 months after date of death6-month extension available
Tax-exempt organization990 / 990-EZ / 990-PFMay 15November 15
A few reminders: An extension gives you more time to file, not more time to pay — any tax owed is still due by the original deadline. Fiscal-year filers and June-30 C-corps follow different dates. Not sure which apply to you? Let's talk.
