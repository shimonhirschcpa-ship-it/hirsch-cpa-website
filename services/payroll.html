Payroll

Accurate, on-time payroll and filings so your team is paid right and your obligations are covered.

What it covers

  • Regular payroll processing and direct deposit
  • Federal, state, and local payroll tax filings
  • Year-end W-2 and 1099 preparation
  • New-hire reporting
  • Payroll set up for new employees or contractors

Who it's for

Businesses with employees or contractors that want payroll run correctly and on schedule, without tracking filing deadlines themselves.

How it works

We set up your payroll schedule, run it each pay period, and handle the associated filings as they come due, so paydays are consistent and your obligations stay current.
