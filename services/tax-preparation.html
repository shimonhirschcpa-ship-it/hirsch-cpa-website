Services / Tax Preparation

Tax Preparation

Returns for individuals, businesses, trusts, and non-profits, prepared, reviewed, and filed.

What it covers

  • Individual income tax returns, including multi-state filings
  • Partnership and S-corporation returns
  • C-corporation returns
  • Trust, estate, and non-profit returns
  • Quarterly estimated tax calculations
  • Extensions and amended returns when needed

Who it's for

Individuals with straightforward or complex returns, business owners filing on behalf of a partnership or corporation, and anyone who wants their personal and business filings coordinated rather than handled in isolation.

How it works

We start with a short conversation about your situation, let you know exactly what documents and figures we need, and prepare your return with a full review before anything is filed. Once filed, your completed return is available to you anytime through the client portal.
