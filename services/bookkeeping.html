Services / Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping

Clean, current books you can trust, with monthly reconciliations and reporting that keep you in control.

What it covers

  • Monthly transaction categorization and bookkeeping
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Monthly financial statements: profit and loss, and balance sheet
  • Clean-up of prior-period books that have fallen behind
  • Books maintained on a schedule that keeps your tax filings on time

Who it's for

Small and mid-sized businesses that want accurate, up-to-date books without hiring an in-house bookkeeper, and business owners who want their books and tax filings handled by the same team.

How it works

We set up a monthly rhythm. Transactions are categorized and reconciled, statements are prepared, and you get a clear picture of where the business stands. When it's time to file, your books are already ready.
