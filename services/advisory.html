Services / Advisory

Advisory

Year-round guidance on entity structure, estimates, and planning. Support in your corner, not just at tax time.

What it covers

  • Entity structure guidance: sole proprietorship, LLC, S-corp, C-corp
  • Tax planning and projections ahead of year-end
  • Estimated tax planning throughout the year
  • Guidance around major business decisions: hiring, expansion, large purchases
  • Ongoing access to a firm that knows your business

Who it's for

Business owners who want a firm they can check in with throughout the year, not just when a return is due, especially ahead of growth, restructuring, or a major transaction.

How it works

We get to know your business and finances, then check in at key points during the year: before major decisions, ahead of estimated payments, and at year-end planning. Tax outcomes get considered before they're locked in, not after.
